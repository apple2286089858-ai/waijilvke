✈️ Immigration Process Step by Step

1

Flight landing
→ Stay Seated

  • ✏️ Carry a pen to fill out landing card/customs declaration.
  • 📱 Open the "Customs Passenger Finger Service" mini program, scan the code to declare in advance faster than paper.
2

Quarantine inspection
→ Scan QR Code

  • 📷 Prepare your Customs Declaration Code (Black Code) and keep passport/boarding pass ready.
3

Border inspection
→ Passport Stamp

  • 🚶 Take the "foreigner's lane" (marked with passport icons/English signs)
6

Leaving the airport
→ Transportation

  • 🏨 Hotel pickup: Reserve in advance by email with the hotel
  • 🚕 Cab: follow the airport signs to the cab queue area/internet car waiting area, see page 6 for details
  • 🚇 Metro: follow airport signs, see page 5 for details
5

Customs inspection
→ Choose the Right Lane

  • 🟢 The inward and outward passengers who have nothing to declare shall choose the "Nothing to Declare Channel" (green channel).
  • 🔴 The inward and outward passengers carrying articles to declare shall choose the "Goods to Declare Channel" (red channel).
4

Baggage claim
→ Check Screens

  • 🏷️ Use bright luggage tags/AirTags for easy identification.

⚡ Airport Tips

  • 📶Free WiFi (passport register, 2h limit)
  • 💴Currency exchange: Poor rates (small amounts only)
  • 🔋Carry power banks to avoid dead phones
  • 📱Activate SIMs via airport WiFi
  • ☎️Emergency: +86-12360 (press 2 for English)
✨

🚫 Prohibited Items

  • 🥩Meat/fruit/seeds (may be confiscated)
  • 🍷Duty-free: 2 cigarettes/alcohol bottles max
🚫

Follow airport signs and instructions for a smooth entry ✨

📶 Internet Access Stay Online

1

📡 Pre-Arrival eSIM Purchase

🔥 Platforms

  • ①Nomad: Wide coverage, flexible plans.
  • ②SimOptions: Very friendly to European users.
  • ③Ditalo: Budget-friendly at Asia Pacific region.

💡 Operating Tips

  • – Order 3 days in advance, receive QR code in your mailbox → Scan to activate the code
  • – Suggest to buy universal package to prevent disconnection.

✅ Pros

  • – Instant connectivity upon landing; no need to change the card.
  • – No ID/real name registration, easy to use Alipay/WeChat
  • – Support multi-country credit card payment

⚠️ Note

  • – Not compatible with some Android phones
  • – Compatible with iPhone XS or later
  • – Slightly more expensive traffic packages (around $5–8 per day)
📱
2

🛬 Buy SIM Cards On Arrival

📍 Locations

  • – Airport counters: Beijing Capital T3, Shanghai Pudong T1, Guangzhou Baiyun T2 and other international airports
  • – Business halls: Recognize "China Mobile" / "China Telecom" / "China Unicom".
📶 China Mobile 📶 China Telecom 📶 China Unicom

🗣 Key Phrases

  • "我要办预付费套餐" → "I want a prepaid SIM card"
  • "最便宜的流量包" → "The cheapest data package"

✅ Pros

  • – On-the-spot activation;
  • – Best value for money.
  • – Choose from three major carriers.

⚠️ Note

  • – Passport required for real-name registration.
  • – Some airport counters close by 10 PM.
  • – Keep SIM packaging (customer hotlines: China Mobile 10086 / China Telecom 10010 / China Unicom 10000).
📶
3
