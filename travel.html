Emergency Handling

Stay safe, stay calm — we've got you covered 🆘🏥🛂










🏥 1. Medical Care Guide Stay Safe~

🚨

In emergencies, dial 120 immediately. Repeat "Hello" to activate English-language assistance (available in Beijing / Shanghai pilot areas). Carry your passport and medical insurance documents to the nearest hospital's Emergency Department.

🚑 (1) Hospital Visit Process

①

Registration

Passport required

Online

Available via hospital websites or WeChat official accounts.

On-Site

Service counter or self-service kiosk → Select department → Receive registration slip.

📋
②

Consultation

Wait in the designated area → Pay attention to the calling system / display screen → Enter the consultation room.

Describe symptoms clearly (use translation apps if needed).

After diagnosis, pay for check-up / medications → Get the medicine / treatments.

🩺
③

Check-up & Treatment

Some tests (blood, ultrasound) require fasting or a full bladder. Confirm in advance.

Hospitalization: Requires family consent (designate an emergency contact beforehand).

💊

💳 (2) Costs & Insurance

Payment Methods

  • – Cash, Alipay, WeChat Pay (some hospitals accept foreign cards).
  • – If an invoice is needed: application at the payment window.

Travel Insurance

  • – Ensure your policy covers medical expenses in China.
  • – Present proof at registration; some hospitals offer direct billing.
🏦

🧘 (3) Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM)

Prescriptions Only

Do not self-administer herbal medicine (may contain unlisted ingredients).

Choose Licensed Clinics

For acupuncture/cupping, avoid unregulated street vendors (sterilization risks).

🌏 Cultural Note

Doctors may ask about age/marital status (for medical records). Major treatment or surgery requires family consent (designate an emergency contact in advance).

🌿

🚨 2. Common Emergencies Be Prepared~

🛂 (1) Lost Documents

Step 1

File a Police Report

– Go to the nearest Police Station (search via maps like Amap).

– Provide passport copies (if available) or alternative ID (e.g. driver's license).

– Complete a Loss Statement and obtain an official stamp.

Step 2

Obtain a Passport
Loss Certificate

– Submit the stamped Loss Statement to the Exit-Entry Administration (under Public Security Bureau).

– Required documents: Passport/visa copies, 2-inch photo.

– Receive the Passport Loss Certificate (valid for 30 days).

Step 3

Renewal of Passport

– Contact your Embassy/Consulate (e.g., U.S. Embassy: +86-10-8531-3000).

– Make an appointment, submit the Passport Loss Certificate, and receive the new passport (3–7 business days).

Emergency Exit: Apply for a Temporary Exit-Entry Permit at the Exit-Entry Administration (processed in 1–2 days).

⭐

Tips

– Store digital copies of passports/visas in email or cloud storage.

– Hotels can provide passport photocopies.

💳 (2) Payment Issues

① Card Payment Failures

  • – Confirm your card's international transactions are enabled (contact your bank).
  • – Try a UnionPay card (some merchants only accept UnionPay).
  • – ATM Withdrawal: Use foreign-friendly ATMs (e.g., Bank of China, CCB) → Insert Visa/Mastercard → Select English → Enter PIN → Withdraw cash.

② Mobile Payment Errors

Alipay / WeChat Pay:

– Check internet connection → Re-link card → Attempt small payments (<¥200, no fee).

Customer Service:

Alipay: +86-571-2688-6000 (English)

WeChat Pay: +86-571-95017 (English)

Backup:

Use cash (exchange at airports/hotels) or visit foreign card-friendly stores (e.g., malls, chain restaurants).

🔍

🌿 (3) Allergy Prevention & Response

Prevention

①

Carry a Chinese Allergy Card stating 「我对＿过敏」 (I am allergic to ＿). e.g. 我对花生 (peanuts) 过敏

②

Keep antihistamines / emergency medication.

Emergency Response

Accidental Ingestion:

Stop eating → Take medication.

Severe Reactions (e.g., difficulty breathing):

Dial 120 → Repeat "Hello" → State "Food allergy emergency."

At the Hospital:

Show your allergy card → Request an English medical record.

Self-Care:

Use Meituan or Amap to locate 24-hour pharmacies → Navigate or order online.
