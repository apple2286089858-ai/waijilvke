Travel & Culture

Explore China like a local — book smart, act right 🎎🏯✨










🏟️ 1. Attraction Bookings Plan Ahead~

📌 Latest Booking Policies

Beijing

Most attractions (Temple of Heaven, Summer Palace) require no reservations except the Forbidden City, National Museum, and Chairman Mao Memorial Hall.

Other Cities

Reservations needed for popular spots (e.g., Shanghai Disney, Terracotta Warriors, West Lake's Broken Bridge).

💡 Book in advance during holidays / weekends.

1 International Platforms

English-friendly · Accepts foreign cards

① Trip.com (Ctrip International)

  • – English interface; instant e-tickets for many attractions.
  • – Supports Visa / Mastercard.

② Klook Travel

  • – Covers 500+ attractions (Great Wall, Terracotta Warriors).
  • – Bilingual e-tickets; scan to enter.

💡 Tips

– Search attractions by English names (e.g., "Forbidden City").

– Select "E-ticket" to skip ticket collection.

🌐

2 Local Apps

Budget-friendly · Requires language switch

① Alipay (Switch to English)

Search attractions by name → Select date → Pay. Supports foreign cards (some attractions may require local payment).

② Meituan (Simple English Mode)

Filter by city → "Attraction Tickets".

③ Dianping (Use Screenshot Translate)

Read reviews → Redirect to Meituan for booking (requires Chinese address input).

📱

3 Official Channels

For in-depth travelers

① WeChat Mini-Programs

  • 🏯 Forbidden City: "Palace Museum" mini-program → Book 7 days in advance (tickets at 8 PM daily). Foreigners: select "Passport Booking" on official website.
  • 🎢 Shanghai Disney: "Shanghai Disney Resort" official account → Foreigner booking channel.
  • 🏺 Terracotta Warriors: "Emperor Qinshihuang's Mausoleum Site Museum" official account → English booking.

② Phone Booking (English Service)

  • 🏯 Forbidden City: +86-10-8500-7062 (press 2 for English)
  • 🎢 Shanghai Disney: +86-21-3158-0000 (English support)
  • Ask hotel staff to assist with bookings (tipping encouraged!).

📋 Booking Tips

✔

Book popular attractions 3–7 days in advance.

✔

Bring your original passport (required for ticket exchange).

✔

Screenshot tickets and save QR codes offline (poor connectivity at sites!).

✔

Use WeChat's "Favorites" to store all booking confirmations.

🎎 2. Cultural Tips Be Respectful~

🤝 (1) Tipping & Etiquette

🍽️

Restaurants / Taxis

No tipping expected.

🏨

Hotel Assistance

Tipping staff for extra help is appreciated.

💰

💬 (2) Communication

😊

Younger locals often speak basic English — ask boldly!

📱

Use translation apps to show Chinese addresses / requests.

🗣️

🚻 (3) Restrooms

Most public restrooms have squat toilets; malls/airports/upscale restaurants offer seated toilets (labeled 「坐便」 or "Toilet Seat").

Squat Toilet Guide:

Stand with feet apart, bend knees slightly, and squat slowly (practice makes perfect!).

Ask staff: "Yǒu zuòbiàn ma?" (有坐便吗？) for seated toilets.

🚽

🥢 (4) Dining Etiquette

✖

Chopsticks — Never stick them upright in rice

(resembles funeral rituals)

✖

Avoid pointing with chopsticks — it's considered rude.
