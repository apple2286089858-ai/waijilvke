Payment & Tax Refund

Pay smart, shop smart, get your refund 💳🧾✈️










💳 1. Payment Methods Pay Anywhere

💳

(1) Card Payments

Physical cards accepted nationwide

✅ Cards Accepted in China

UnionPay, Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Diners Club, Discover.

Look for corresponding logos at merchants. If unsure, ask staff if your card is accepted.

⭐ Tips

UnionPay cards are widely accepted at all card terminals nationwide.

💳

No fees for transactions under ¥200  ·  3% fee for transactions ≥¥200

WeChat Pay 微信支付

Customer Service: +86-400 670 0700

Step 1 · Download

Install WeChat and update to the latest version.

Step 2 · Register

Use an international number and complete identity verification.

Step 3 · Enable WeChat Pay

Go to 「Me」→「Services」→「Wallet」.

If missing, activate via 「Me」→「Settings」→「General」→「Assistive Features」→「Enable WeChat Pay」.

Supported: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Diners Club, Discover.

Step 5 · Two Payment Methods

📷

Option 1

Scan the merchant's green QR code

📲

Show your payment code or request payment

💵 2. Cash Exchange RMB Ready~

📄 Required Documents

  • • Original valid passport (copies might not be accepted)
  • • Foreign cash (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.)
  • • International debit/credit card (Visa/Mastercard)
①

Currency Exchange Counters

Found at airports, international hotels.

Exchange foreign cash to RMB.

🏦
②

Bank Branches

1
Locate a Branch: Use maps (e.g., Amap) to find banks with "Foreign Exchange" signs. Check operating hours.
2
Fill Form: Complete the Foreign Exchange Application Form with: name (as in passport), passport number, exchange amount, contact info (optional).
3
Submit Documents: Hand over passport, foreign cash, and form. Staff will confirm the rate and process the exchange.
4
Receive RMB: Verify the amount and keep the receipt.
🏦
③

ATM Withdrawals

Supported Banks: Bank of China, China Construction Bank, ICBC (some ATMs)
①

Find ATMs with English interfaces.

②

Insert card, select language.

③

Choose "Withdrawal", enter amount (note daily limits).

④

Enter PIN, confirm.

⑤

Collect cash, card, and receipt.

Fees: Up to bank. Some ATMs reject small withdrawals (<¥1,200).

⭐ Note: Exchange Limits

Max. ¥20,000 per person per transaction.
Exceeding limits requires additional proof (visa, itinerary).

🧾 3. Tax Refund on Departure Get Money Back!

📋 Policy & Conditions

Policy:

VAT (Value-added Tax) refunds for foreign visitors (residing ≤183 days) on purchases from tax-free shops.

✅ Conditions

  • • Single-day purchases ≥¥500 at the same store.
  • • Items unused/unconsumed.
  • • Departure within 90 days of purchase.
  • • Items carried or checked in upon departure.

📊 Refund Calculation

Refund = (Invoice amount including VAT) × Refund rate

  • • 11% for 13% VAT items
  • • 8% for 9% VAT items
  • • Agency fee: 2% deducted

🪜 Refund Steps

①

Obtain Form & Invoice

Get a Tax Refund Application Form and invoice from the store.

②

Declare at Customs

Declare items at customs before departure. Present items, form, and invoice for inspection.

③

Receive Refund

Cash (≤¥10,000) or bank transfer (>¥10,000).

🌴 4. Hainan Island Duty-Free Policy Shop Smart~

✅ Eligibility

Travelers aged 16+ with valid ID (passport for foreigners) departing Hainan via air/train/ship (not leaving China).

📊 Annual Quota

¥100,000 per person (≈ €13,000), unlimited purchases.

🛍️ Purchase & Pickup

Buy at duty-free shops/online, collect at designated airport/train/port areas.

🔗 Learn More

National Tax Administration: https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/
