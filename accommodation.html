1

Download

Get the app or use the web version

🍎

iOS users: Search Trip.com in the App Store.

🤖

Android users: Search Trip.com on Google Play.

💻

Web version: Visit https://www.trip.com

It is allowed to switch languages and currencies.

📱
2

Register an Account

Quick & easy sign-up

①

Open the app / website → Click "Sign Up" → Choose email or international phone number.

②

Fill in your details → Enter the verification code → Complete registration.

💡 Tips

  • – Link your Google account for quicker login.
  • – Ensure your profile name matches your passport.
📝
3

Identity Verification

Passport verification for foreign guests

Verification path:

「Manual Verification」 → 「For Non-Mainland Chinese
(HK, Macau, Taiwan & Overseas)」 → 「Passport Verification」

Follow the instructions to set a payment password and submit your passport details and wait for confirmation.

🪪
4

Search for Hotels

Find accommodation that accepts foreign guests

①

Enter basic search criteria (location / dates / guests).

②

Filter results:

Ctrip (China Mainland version): Click 「Filters」→ Check 「Accepts Foreign Guests」
Trip.com (International version): No need for this filter.
🔍
5

Select Room & Payment

Review details, fill in guest info, and pay securely

① Check Room Details

Review room descriptions (breakfast inclusion, cancellation policy, etc.).

Note: Look for the 「Foreign Guests Allowed」 label and requirements (e.g., passport copies).

② Guest Information

Enter guest information as shown on your passport.

③ Payment

Accepted cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, etc.

Click 「Add Payment Method」 → Enter card number / expiry date / CVV.

★ Some hotels allow "Pay at Property" but may require a pre-authorization hold.
💳
💡

Tips

📋 Modify a Booking:

Go to 「My Bookings」→ Select your order → Click 「Modify」 to adjust dates / room type.

📞 Customer Support:

Contact Trip.com's 24/7 English support: +1-800-210-6567
