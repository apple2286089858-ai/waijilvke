Transportation & Navigation

Getting around China made simple 🚄🚇🛵










🚆 1. Transportation Getting Around

🚇 Metro

✅ Ticketing Options

① Self-service Machines

Select English interface → Choose destination on the route map → Make payment → Collect ticket.

Ticket Office: Show passport → Make payment.

② E-Ticket (QR Code)

Open Alipay → Click "Transport" → Select "City" → Activate the local "Metro QR Code" → Scan to enter/exit.

③ Metro Passes

Ticket Office: Show passport → Make payment.

⚠️ Note

  • – Tap in/out twice (once at entry, once at exit).
  • – Passport holders are advised to use staffed gates to avoid scanner issues.
🚇

🚄 High-Speed Rail (HSR)

✅ Ticketing Options

① Online Booking (Recommended)

Trip.com (International Version):

English interface → Enter route (e.g., Beijing→Shanghai) → Select train → Fill in passport details → Pay with Visa/Mastercard.

E-tickets: Scan QR code at the gate (no paper ticket needed!).

www.12306.cn:

Register → Passport verification → Show passport at the gate.

② Offline Booking

Visit HSR station counters → Show passport → Pay by cash/card.

✅ Boarding Process

①
Enter the Station – Use self-service gates to scan your passport.
②
Security Check – Queue for security screening after entering.
③
Find Your Gate – Check your ticket (via app) for the assigned gate. Wait nearby until boarding begins.
④
Board the Train – Scan passport at gate when "Now Boarding" displays.
⑤
Locate Your Seat – Match ticket to number above windows (e.g., 06A = Row 6, Seat A).
🚄

🚖 Ride-Hailing (Didi Rider)

📱 Recommended App: Didi Rider

✅ Steps

①

Download "Didi Rider" → Register with your international number.

②

Bind Visa/Mastercard → Set payment code.

③

Input destination (English/Chinese) → Select model (Economy/Comfort) → Confirm pickup point.

Assist the driver by dragging the map marker and sending text notes, e.g. "Near Starbucks".

④

Share your real-time trip with friends via the app.

⚠️ Note

  • – Verify license plate before boarding.
  • – In-app audio recording is enabled by default.

  • – Full English interface, navigate by chatting with Siri

Petal Maps

By Huawei · Global Navigation

✅ Pros

  • – Huawei phones come pre-installed, other Androids can install it too
  • – Free switching between Chinese and English, real-time road conditions
  • – Offline map function — don't panic even without internet connection

🟡 Note

  • – Non-Huawei phones need to manually download the installation package
  • – Information may be incomplete for rural areas

⭐ Tips

  • – Huawei users directly poke [App Market] to download
  • – APKPure is recommended for third-party Android installations.
